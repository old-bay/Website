Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about UMBC HvZ

  • Humans vs. Zombies (HvZ) is a recreational game of moderated tag played at UMBC. It combines elements of Nerf wars, manhunt, tag, and capture the flag with unique mechanics. Players are divided into humans and zombies, with humans trying to complete objectives while zombies try to tag them. It's a great way to make friends, get some exercise, and have a blast on campus!

  • Missions are held on Mondays and Thursdays at 7:00 PM, typically starting at SOND 109. Community meetings are held every Sunday at 1:00 PM in SOND 109 (while spring & fall classes are in session). You can also join our Discord server for online participation and announcements.

  • Just show up to a mission or community meeting! There's no sign-up required to start playing our regular weekly games. If you're new, let an officer know and they'll help get you started. You can also join our Discord server to stay in the loop.

  • You'll need a brightly colored bandanna (provided by the club if you don't have one) and equipment such as rolled socks or NERF darts for stunning zombies. Please note that toy blasters are currently banned on UMBC campus for normal gameplay — only throwables and throwing darts are allowed. Check the rules page for the full equipment list.

  • A weeklong is a special week-long event where the game of HvZ extends beyond our normal mission hours. During a weeklong, the game is active throughout the day on campus — humans must watch their backs at all times! Weeklongs have their own special rules and are announced in advance. They typically happen once per semester and are one of our most popular events.

  • The invitational is our annual event where we invite HvZ clubs from other colleges to come play with us at UMBC. It's a great opportunity to meet players from other schools and experience different play styles. Registration for the invitational is handled separately — check our home page for the latest registration link.

  • Officers run the club and organize missions. Subofficers are experienced players who assist the officers during gameplay — they can make calls on deaths, holds, and other in-game rulings even while playing. If you believe a subofficer has made an incorrect call, please contact the officers. Visit the Meet the Admins page to see the current officer board and subofficer roster.

  • Modded NERF/Buzz Bee/Worker darts and discs are allowed, but they are subject to officer approval. If you're unsure whether your equipment is allowed, bring it to a mission and ask an officer before gameplay begins. Safety is our top priority, so modifications that make equipment dangerous will not be approved.

  • In addition to regular gameplay, you can earn achievements for noteworthy in-game accomplishments. Achievements come in four tiers: Basic, Recruit, Veteran, and the prestigious Legendary class. All achievements must be verified by a moderator. Check the Achievement Database for the full list.

  • We love community-submitted missions! You can submit mission feedback and ideas using our mission feedback form. If you want to create and submit a full mission, email the officers at umbchvzofficers@gmail.com with your slides.

Still Have Questions?

Email the officers at umbchvzofficers@gmail.com or ask in our Discord server.
