Welcome to the New Website

Posted by Web Committee April 8, 2026

Welcome to the newly redesigned UMBC HvZ website! We've rebuilt the site from the ground up with modern web technologies for a better experience on all devices. The new site features responsive design, improved accessibility, and a cleaner look.

If you notice any issues or have suggestions, please reach out to the Web Committee.

Blaster Policy Update

Posted by Officer Board January 15, 2026

ALL TOY BLASTERS are currently banned on UMBC campus as it regards our normal gameplay. Please do not bring a blaster to a club meeting as we will have to ask you to put it away and return it to your car or dorm.

Our normal gameplay will for now consist of throwables and throwing darts. We are working on resolving this. Thank you for your patience.

Invitational Registration Open
