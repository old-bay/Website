Rules

General Rules

  1. Always follow UMBC campus policies and local laws.
  2. No gameplay inside buildings, parking garages, or the library.
  3. No physical contact — tag by hitting with a Nerf dart or sock ball.
  4. All blasters must be inspected and approved by officers.
  5. No modified blasters that could cause injury.
  6. All players must have a waiver on file each year.
  7. Moderator decisions are final during missions.

Humans

  • Stun zombies by hitting them with a dart or sock ball.
  • Stunned zombies must wait the stun timer before resuming play.
  • Humans win by completing mission objectives or surviving.
  • If tagged by a zombie, you become a zombie.

Zombies

  • Tag humans to grow the horde.
  • When stunned, stand still for the stun timer duration.
  • Zombies cannot use blasters or throw socks.
  • Zombies win by tagging all humans or completing zombie objectives.

Equipment

  • Nerf blasters: Must fire standard Nerf darts only. Must be inspected.
  • Sock balls: Rolled-up socks. Single socks only, no weighted socks.
  • Bandannas: Worn on the arm (human) or head (zombie) during weeklongs.
